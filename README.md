# funlabs.vibe

**Make something every day. Play is the highest form of research.**

funlabs.vibe is a space for turning ideas into playful experiments. Start
small, make the idea real, learn from what happens, and keep going. Not every
experiment needs to become a product; every experiment can teach you something.

## The practice

- Pick an idea and make a small version of it today.
- Play with it, share it, or try it out.
- Notice what you learned, then follow the next idea.
